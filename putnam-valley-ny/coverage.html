How we decide what counts

We write from official recordings and transcripts. For bodies that publish only agendas and minutes, we say so and report what the documents show, which is less than a recording would. A meeting appears on this page as covered only after its recap is published. If you know of a meeting we're missing, or one you'd like covered next, tell us by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository.

Meeting dates come from the town's and the school district's published schedules and notices and are subject to change. Check the Town Board page, the town calendar or the school district's Board of Education page before you plan to attend.
