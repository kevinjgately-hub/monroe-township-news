What we cover, and what we don't yet

Our goal is every public meeting of Putnam Valley’s town government and school board. We are just getting started, and we would rather show you exactly where the gaps are than imply otherwise. So far our stories come from written records: minutes, notices and the district’s website. We have not yet recapped any meeting from its video. This page is updated whenever we publish. Smaller boards and committees

We found no agendas or minutes posted by any of the town’s smaller bodies. Our boards roundup reports what the Supervisor’s newsletters and Town Board minutes say about the Commission of Lakes, the Climate Smart Task Force and the Comprehensive Plan Steering Committee. The Advisory Board on Architecture & Community Appearance, Board of Assessment Review, Board of Ethics, Commission for the Conservation of the Environment, Parks & Recreation Commission, Town Safety Committee and Roaring Brook Lake Preservation Committee are not covered.

How we decide what counts

We write from official recordings, minutes and records, and every story says which it used. When all we have is an agenda or notice, we report it as a plan, not a result. If you know of a meeting we're missing, or one you'd like covered next, tell us by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository.

Meeting dates come from the town's and the school district's published schedules and notices and are subject to change. Check the Town Board page, the town calendar or the school district's Board of Education page before you plan to attend.
