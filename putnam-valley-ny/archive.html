Town BoardNov 25, 2026

What Putnam Valley's Town Board has put on the record this fall: a tax-cap override, two moratoriums and a Verizon deal

The board adopts a law letting the 2027 budget exceed the state tax cap, schedules November 18 hearings on temporary bans on data centers and on large battery-storage and wind projects, and sets a December hearing on a cable deal that could bring Verizon Fios to about 5,000 homes.

Planning BoardNov 25, 2026

What's been in front of Putnam Valley's Planning Board in 2026: a seminary cemetery, a YMCA camp and a karate school

A three-phase cemetery and chapel on 63 acres off Barger Street, renovations at YMCA Camp Combe, a commercial building on Peekskill Hollow Road, a karate school and several short-term rentals are among the applications on the board's posted agendas from March through July.

Zoning Board of AppealsNov 25, 2026

What Putnam Valley's Zoning Board of Appeals has heard in 2026: lakefront decks, a fire-destroyed home and YMCA Camp Combe

Most requests are for decks, pools, sheds and additions on small lakeside lots, plus a rebuild of a Walnut Road home destroyed by fire and a set of variances for YMCA Camp Combe that was held over from June to July.

Board of EducationNov 25, 2026

Putnam Valley's school board this year: a $65.3 million budget, a new trustee and a fall of school reports

Voters passed the 2026–27 budget 985 to 876 in May and elected Jay Lee to the board. This fall the board has heard opening-of-school reports from all three schools, a look at the senior citizen tax exemption and new achievement data. Its next meeting is December 10.

Boards & CommissionsNov 25, 2026

Putnam Valley's lakes commission, Climate Smart task force and comprehensive-plan committee: what the town has said about them

The Commission of Lakes is weighing algae fixes for Lake Peekskill and wakeboat rules for Lake Oscawana, the town is applying for a three-year study of toxic algae flowing toward Peekskill's water supply, and a resident committee is starting work on the first comprehensive-plan update since 2007.

Everything in the minutes of Putnam Valley's July 15 Town Board meeting

The board buys two electric-vehicle chargers for Town Hall with a $29,822 grant, pays $332,873 toward the Cimarron Road bridge, fixes a salary typo in the union contract, and hears about senior scams, summer school construction and a lost trail grant.
