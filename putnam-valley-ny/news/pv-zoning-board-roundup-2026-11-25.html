Zoning Board of Appeals · Written-Records Roundup

What Putnam Valley's Zoning Board of Appeals has heard in 2026: lakefront decks, a fire-destroyed home and YMCA Camp Combe

Most requests are for decks, pools, sheds and additions on small lakeside lots, plus a rebuild of a Walnut Road home destroyed by fire and a set of variances for YMCA Camp Combe that was held over from June to July.

Nov 25, 2026 · Compiled from Zoning Board of Appeals hearing notices posted on the town website, not from recordings · 5 min read

Quick facts

What
Applications on the ZBA's public hearing notices for April 23, May 21, June 25 and July 30, 2026
Who
A five-member board chaired by William Maskiell
When it meets
A monthly public hearing on a Thursday at 6:30 p.m. at Town Hall
How to read this
Notices list requests, not decisions. The town has posted video of the January 29, April 23, June 25, July 30 and September 24 hearings, which we have not yet recapped.

The Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) hears from property owners whose plans were turned down by the building inspector because they break a zoning rule. It can grant a variance, permission to depart from a rule, usually on setbacks (how close a building can be to a property line or a lake), height or lot coverage (how much of a lot can be built on). Many of Putnam Valley's homes sit on small lots around Lake Peekskill, Lake Oscawana and other lakes, which is why so many requests involve decks, docks and lakefront setbacks. Several requests cite Section 165-44 A(1) of the zoning code, which this year's notices pair with height. We describe private homeowners' applications by street rather than by name.

1. YMCA Camp Combe asks for a bundle of variances

YMCA Camp Combe at 684 Peekskill Hollow Road, in the PD zone, first appeared on the June 25 notice asking for side, front and lot coverage variances, variances from code sections on setbacks from the property line and distance from neighboring structures, and variances for maximum building length. The request was "held over" to July 30, meaning the board did not finish with it in June. The camp's renovation plan is also before the Planning Board (see our Planning Board roundup).

2. Rebuilding a home destroyed by fire

On April 23 the board heard a request for side, front, lot coverage and height variances to rebuild a home on Walnut Road, in the Lake Peekskill (LP) zone, that the notice says was destroyed by fire. The same property was on the Planning Board's agenda in March for reconstruction of the house.

3. Decks, docks, pools and sheds

  • April 23 (nine new applications): an addition and deck on Floradan Road; decks on James Drive, West Shore Drive (a lake setback), Lake Drive (a new, larger lakeside deck and stairs) and Brookdale Road; an in-ground pool on Lincoln Road; a pool, pavilion and basketball court on Cranberry Pond Road; and a dormer raising the roof on Oakdale Road.
  • May 21: the Floradan Road addition, held over from April; lakefront variances for a deck and attached dock on Lake Shore Road; and a 6-foot fence on Lakeview Drive. The town's video page lists no May hearing, so it may not have gone ahead as planned.
  • June 25 (nine new applications): the Lake Shore Road deck and dock again, a new entryway on Lake Drive, sheds on Stephen Smith Drive and Wood Street, a front entryway on Canopus Hollow Road, pools on West Shore Drive and Meyer Drive, an addition on Wood Street, and the YMCA request.
  • July 30: the YMCA request, held over; a railing and pergola on an existing patio on Tanglewylde Road; and a shed on Watson Way.

Earlier in 2026, a February 26 hearing included a second-story addition on Woodleigh Road, and another early-2026 notice listed a front-yard setback variance for the proposed 100-by-51-foot commercial building at 824 Peekskill Hollow Road.

What we don't have

The town does not post ZBA decisions or minutes on its website, and we found no hearing notices after July 30, though video of a September 24 hearing is posted. To learn how a request was decided, watch the town's recording or contact the ZBA clerk at 845-526-3740.
