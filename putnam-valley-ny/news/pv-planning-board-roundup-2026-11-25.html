Planning Board · Written-Records Roundup

What's been in front of Putnam Valley's Planning Board in 2026: a seminary cemetery, a YMCA camp and a karate school

A three-phase cemetery and chapel on 63 acres off Barger Street, renovations at YMCA Camp Combe, a commercial building on Peekskill Hollow Road, a karate school and several short-term rentals are among the applications on the board's posted agendas from March through July.

Nov 25, 2026 · Compiled from Planning Board meeting notices posted on the town website, not from recordings · 6 min read

Quick facts

What
Applications listed on the Planning Board's meeting notices for March 30, April 27, June 8 and July 13, 2026
Who
A five-member board chaired by Dale Phillips, advised by the town planner, engineer and planning counsel
When it meets
Monday evenings at 6 p.m. at Town Hall, 265 Oscawana Lake Road
How to read this
The notices are agendas: they list what the board planned to review, not what it decided. We found no posted minutes and no notices after July 13.

The Planning Board decides whether new buildings, subdivisions and major site work can go ahead, and under what conditions. It reviews site plans (drawings of how a property will be built and used), issues special use permits for uses the zoning allows only with board approval, and grants the town's wetland and major grading permits for work near wetlands or involving large amounts of earth. Most applications come back several times for "review" before a public hearing, where neighbors can speak, and a vote. The town posts a notice before each meeting listing what is on the agenda; it does not post the minutes, so this story reports what was scheduled. We name applicants that are organizations or businesses, and describe private homeowners' applications by street.

1. A cemetery, chapel and garage planned on Barger Street

The largest application on the 2026 agendas is from the Rabbinical Seminary of America, which wants to build a cemetery, an access road, parking, a chapel building, a garage and other improvements on about 62.79 acres on Barger Street, in three phases. The land is split between the R-2 residential zone and the PD (planned development) zone. The project needs site plan approval, a special use permit, a major grading permit and a wetland permit. It was listed for review on March 30 and again on April 27. The notices do not give building sizes or describe the phases. The same file was on the board's agenda in July 2025 for soil testing near a former gas station on the property.

2. Renovations at YMCA Camp Combe

YMCA Camp Combe, on about 79.2 acres at 684 Peekskill Hollow Road, was on the March 30 agenda seeking an amended site plan, an amended special use permit, and grading and wetland permits to renovate its existing facilities "to enhance the permitted uses." The camp also needs several variances from the Zoning Board of Appeals, including for building length and distance between structures; see our ZBA roundup.

3. A commercial building on Peekskill Hollow Road

An applicant listed as Peekskill Hollow Preserve wants to build a 100-by-51-foot commercial building on about 1.34 acres at 824 Peekskill Hollow Road, in the CN (neighborhood commercial) zone. On March 30 the board was to consider declaring itself lead agency, meaning it would take charge of the project's environmental review under the state's environmental quality law. The same site was on the agenda in November 2025 under a different applicant name, and the project has also sought a front-yard setback variance from the Zoning Board.

4. Sports fields and a karate school

FC Putnam Sports, listed with Josal, asked on April 27 to reinstate a previously approved special use permit for about 13.5 acres at 7 Bryant Pond Road. The notice does not say what the permit covered or why it lapsed. On June 8 the board held a public hearing on Go No Sen Karate's request for a special use permit to run a karate training facility on about 1.5 acres at 17 Peekskill Hollow Road.

5. Short-term rentals

As these notices show, a short-term rental of a home in Putnam Valley needs a special use permit from the Planning Board. Public hearings to legalize existing short-term rentals were scheduled for homes on Lovers Lane and Point Drive South on March 30 and on Cayuga Road on April 27. (The March 30 notice lists the Cayuga Road application with the Lovers Lane address and acreage, which looks like a copying error.)

6. Homes, a subdivision and soil cleanups

  • Subdivision: a public hearing on June 8 on splitting about 4.05 acres on Finnerty Place into two lots, one keeping the existing house and one for a new house.
  • New and rebuilt homes: a house with well, septic and pool on about 6.67 acres on West Shore Drive (public hearing April 27); a house on about 5.21 acres on Trading Post Lane, which has been under review since August 2025 and was on the July 13 agenda again; a teardown and rebuild with a separate garage on Barger Street; and a new house on about two acres on Wood Street (June 8 and July 13).
  • Backyard projects: a public hearing on June 8 on replacing a pool and adding a retaining wall, pavilion and basketball court on Cranberry Pond Road, a project that also needed zoning variances.
  • Soil remediation: cleanup plans for a property on Sprout Brook Road (March 30) and for soil placed outside the approved site plan on Oscawana Lake Road (July 13).
  • Extensions: on July 13 the board was asked to extend earlier approvals for properties on Peekskill Hollow Road, West Shore Drive and Living Springs Lane. An extension keeps an approval alive for an applicant who has not yet started or finished work.

What we don't have

The town does not post Planning Board minutes or resolutions on its website, so we cannot say which of these applications were approved. We found no meeting notices after July 13. At the Town Board's July 15 meeting, officials said the town's own logging (forest management) project would go to the next Planning Board meeting. The Planning Board clerk's office at Town Hall (845-526-3740) can provide decisions and application files.
