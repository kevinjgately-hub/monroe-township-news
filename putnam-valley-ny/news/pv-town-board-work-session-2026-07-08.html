Town Board · Work Session Recap

Everything in the minutes of Putnam Valley's July 8 Town Board work session

With two members absent, the board puts EV charger installation out to bid, updates the rental contract for the Lake Peekskill Community Center, and approves day camp pay for about 55 staff in a 50-minute session.

Jul 8, 2026 · Compiled from the official minutes posted on the town website, not from the recording · 3 min read

Quick facts

What
Work session of the Putnam Valley Town Board. Work sessions are public meetings where the board can and does vote.
When
Wednesday, July 8, 2026, 5 p.m.; adjourned 5:50 p.m.
Who
Supervisor Alison Jolicoeur and Council Members Christian Russo and Louie Luongo present; Sherry Howard and CJ Brooks absent
Votes
All eight resolutions passed unanimously among the three members present

Councilman Luongo opened with a moment of silence for those who fought for the nation's freedoms, ahead of the 250th anniversary of the United States.

1. EV chargers go out to bid

The board authorized seeking bids to install two zero-emission vehicle charging stations at the Town Hall campus, 265 Oscawana Lake Road, including concrete footings, an electric meter, four concrete-filled steel posts (bollards) to protect them, and the electrical work needed for permits. The minutes say the money comes from a zero-emission vehicle grant the town received last year. A week later the board approved buying the chargers themselves (see our July 15 recap).

2. A new rental contract for the Lake Peekskill Community Center

The board approved an updated license agreement for people renting the Lake Peekskill Community Center. It covers rental fees, security deposits, liability insurance, cancellations and following town rules, and staff will use it for all future reservations. The minutes do not list the fees.

3. Summer staffing

  • Day camp salaries for about 55 staff, including senior recreation leaders, recreation specialists and assistants, bus drivers and lifeguards, at hourly rates from $13.50 to $31.
  • A beach monitor for the Lake Peekskill Improvement District from July 1 through Labor Day weekend, hourly with no benefits.
  • $400 for beach setup at Lookout Manor ($150) and setup and weed removal at the Barger Pond swimming area ($250), charged to those districts.
  • One personnel change: a childcare assistant position (listed as "PVCC") at $15.50 an hour.

4. Routine business

The board accepted the minutes of its June 3 and June 17 meetings and the Building Department's fee report for June. There was no public comment. The board went into a closed-door executive session on personnel matters and took no action afterward. The next meeting was the regular meeting on July 15.
