About this site

Putnam Valley in Focus covers the local government of the Town of Putnam Valley, in Putnam County, New York: the Town Board, the Planning Board, the Zoning Board of Appeals and the Putnam Valley Central School District Board of Education. The name is the promise: every meeting, every decision, in focus. We are not there yet, and our Coverage page shows exactly what we have and haven't covered. Town and school board meetings can run for hours, full of procedural language, local-law numbers and references that assume you already know the backstory. Most people don't have that time. This site tries to close that gap.

How stories are made

Each story starts from an official meeting recording or the town's or district's own records. The meeting is reviewed and condensed into plain language, with technical terms (local law, resolution, variance, site plan, tax levy and so on) explained the first time they come up rather than assumed. Nothing is added that wasn't said at the meeting or shown in the record; where a speaker made a claim that hasn't been independently verified, the story says so.

What this site is not

This is not an official publication of the Town of Putnam Valley, the Putnam Valley Central School District, Putnam County, or any government body, and it has no connection to any of them. It is not a substitute for official minutes, which are the legal record of what a board did. If you need to rely on something (a vote outcome, a legal deadline, a dollar figure), verify it against the town's website or contact the Town Clerk's office directly.

Privacy

Reading the site sets no cookies and does not track individual readers. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused tool that counts page views and visits in aggregate so we can see which stories are being read. It does not collect personal information, build visitor profiles or follow readers around the web.

The support link takes you to Buy Me a Coffee, a separate service with its own privacy policy. Nothing from that service is loaded on this site, and we do not receive your payment details.

Supporting this project

Putnam Valley in Focus is part of Towns in Focus, which is free to read and carries no advertising. It is an independent project run by one person, and readers who want to help cover its costs can do so through Buy Me a Coffee. Support does not buy influence over coverage, and it is not tax-deductible, because this is not a registered nonprofit.

Corrections

Mistakes happen, especially when condensing long meetings into a few minutes of reading. If something here is wrong or misleading, please open an issue on this site's GitHub repository so it can be fixed.
