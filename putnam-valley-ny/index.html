Putnam Valley in Focus is here

Plain-language recaps of the Town of Putnam Valley’s local government: the Town Board, the Planning Board, the Zoning Board of Appeals and the Putnam Valley Central School District Board of Education. Each story is built from the town’s and the district’s own meeting recordings and records, with the jargon explained as we go.

Our first recaps are on the way. Until then, the Coverage page lists every body we plan to follow, when it meets and where its records are posted.

Wednesday, December 16, 2026 · 6:00 p.m.

Putnam Valley Town Hall, 265 Oscawana Lake Road

What's on the agenda

The town has noticed a public hearing that evening on a proposed cable television franchise agreement with Verizon New York. The full agenda typically appears on the Town Board page shortly before the meeting.

Town Board agendas & documents →

Town Board meetings are open to the public and are shown live on the town’s Channel 20, with video posted on the town website. The board usually holds a 5 p.m. work session on the second Wednesday of the month and its regular 6 p.m. meeting on the third Wednesday. Dates and agenda items are subject to change.
