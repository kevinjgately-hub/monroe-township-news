Town Board agendas & documents →

Town Board meetings are open to the public and are shown on the town’s Channel 20, with video posted on the town website. The board usually holds a 5 p.m. work session on the second Wednesday of the month and its regular 6 p.m. meeting on the third Wednesday. Dates and agenda items are subject to change.
