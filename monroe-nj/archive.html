Boards & CommissionsOct 18, 2026

What Monroe's Environmental Commission, Library Board and Affordable Housing Board have posted lately

A solar company's variance request is on the Environmental Commission's October agenda, the Library Board installs pick-up lockers and prepares a lending-policy vote, and the Affordable Housing Board's posted record stops in May. Here is what these three boards have put on the township website.

Written-Records Roundup

Town CouncilOct 5, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's October 5 council meeting

The old landfill's sale to a solar developer is approved after a pointed debate over who pays, a $1.88 million bond finally passes, a study of four Applegarth Road lots raises questions, and the township gets a clean audit.

Zoning BoardSep 29, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's September 29 Zoning Board meeting

A gun shop at the Recoil shooting range wins approval to sell firearms and ammunition, the new Chipotle on Route 33 is approved after a long argument over parking and a bike lane on a private road, and the board formalizes one earlier decision.

Planning BoardSep 24, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's September 24 Planning Board meeting

A short meeting with no new applications: the board puts a wooded subdivision's curb-and-sidewalk waiver in writing, a resident asks about a development near Butcher Road, and the chairman opens with a moment of silence for a longtime township public servant.

Board of EducationSep 16, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's September 16 Board of Education meeting

The board approves a three-year teachers' contract with raises of 4.5%, 4% and 4%, formally invokes the "doctrine of necessity" so members with conflicts can vote on matters involving the superintendent, and hears a heated argument over how a law firm was hired.

Town CouncilSep 2, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's September 2 council meeting

The landfill-to-solar sale clears its first vote, a bond ordinance that failed in August comes back and clears a first vote, the town's fight with its old insurance fund comes up in public comment, and a $5.7 million water-treatment contract is approved.

Board of EducationAug 19, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's August 19 Board of Education meeting

The superintendent remains on paid leave while the board voted to investigate possible tenure charges against her, a new statewide cell phone ban takes effect this school year, and full-day kindergarten is officially here.

Town CouncilAug 3, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's August 3 council meeting

Data centers effectively banned, the old landfill on its way to becoming a solar farm, $4.4 million in water and sewer borrowing approved while a $2.4 million general bond failed, three new police officers, and everything else from the meeting.

Planning BoardJul 23, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's July 23 Planning Board meeting

A zoning tweak that's really about heading off data centers, the old landfill's solar redevelopment plan clears a step with new answers about who's on the hook financially, and a wooded subdivision's paperwork finally gets sorted out.

Zoning BoardJun 30, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's June 30 Zoning Board meeting

A federal security grant is paying for a taller fence around a Sikh house of worship, a long-planned Applegarth Road subdivision clears its last hurdle, and one application gets carried to next month after a family death.

Town CouncilJun 29, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's June 29 council meeting

Data centers, a landfill solar plan and about $6.8 million in borrowing all take their first step, residents press the council on data centers and a worn-out community center rink, and the engineer lays out a summer of road and rail-crossing work.

Town CouncilJun 3, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's June 3 council meeting

The council approves construction of a roughly $3.2 million indoor sports building at James Monroe Park amid questions about the police station expansion, officials say there are no data centers or data center applications in town, and the engineer maps out a summer of paving and a stalled county intersection.

Town CouncilMay 4, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's May 4 council meeting

The 2026 municipal budget is adopted, water and sewer fees go up, a Route 33 affordable housing site gets looser buffer rules, and residents press the council on electric bills, bus access and a request to waive a permit fee.

Town CouncilApr 6, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's April 6 council meeting

The mayor presents a $79.5 million budget with a 4.3-cent tax rate increase, the council adopts a $2.4 million road bond and hears how much the township owes, the regional watershed study gets more expensive, and residents ask for lower gun permit fees and a light pollution law.

Town CouncilMar 2, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's March 2 council meeting

A divided council adopts the rezoning that implements Monroe's fourth-round affordable housing plan, including 30 wheelchair-accessible apartments at the former Meadow View Care Center that neighbors fought, as commuters ask for a bus to Jersey City and the town digs out from a blizzard.

Town CouncilFeb 9, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's February 9 council meeting

The power company answers for three outages and a street light backlog, the council holds its required public hearing on a new $1.9 million park off North Disbrow Hill Road, four affordable housing ordinances pass first reading, and the town takes stock of a storm that left three weeks of ice.

Town CouncilJan 5, 2026

Everything that happened at Monroe's January 5 council meeting

Rupa Siegel is elected council president on a 4–1 vote and Michael Markel vice president, three longtime employees retire, the council moves a planned solar site toward a redevelopment plan as a resident questions the public notice, and the new police dispatch center opens.
