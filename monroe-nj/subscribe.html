Get Monroe in Focus by email

New recaps of Monroe Township council, school board, planning and zoning meetings, sent to your inbox when they are published. Each one is written in plain language from the official meeting recording, so you can follow what was decided without sitting through the meeting.

Free, and you can unsubscribe at any time with one click. You will get a confirmation email first; click the link in it to finish signing up. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your junk or spam folder and mark it as not junk, so future recaps reach your inbox. We use your email address only to send Monroe in Focus updates and never sell or share the list. The form is provided by MailerLite and protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which may set cookies on this page; details are in the privacy section.

Before you rely on anything here

Articles are compiled from official public meeting recordings and township records. Always verify against the official meeting video or the township's own minutes before relying on anything here for a decision.
