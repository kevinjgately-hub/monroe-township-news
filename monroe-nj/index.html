Around Monroe

Practical news from official sources · Updated Oct 15, 2026

Early voting for the Nov. 3 general election starts Oct. 24 at the Monroe library

Early voting runs Oct. 24 through Nov. 1 at the Monroe Township Library, 4 Municipal Plaza — Monday through Saturday 10 a.m. to 8 p.m., Sunday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. The Oct. 13 registration deadline has passed. If you want a mail-in ballot, applications sent by mail must reach the Middlesex County Clerk seven days before the election (Oct. 27), and a mailed ballot must be sent by Election Day and reach the county within six days.

Source: Township of Monroe elections page and Monroe Township Public Library

Free small-business workshop at the Community Center on Oct. 27

"Business Resources for Success," put on with Middlesex County's economic development department, runs 9 to 11:30 a.m. Topics include small-business grants and loans, free technical assistance and mentoring, and how to certify to do business with New Jersey governments.

Source: announced at the Oct. 5 council meeting (our recap)

Fall events: Oktoberfest, a farm family night, and the township's Diwali celebration

Oktoberfest is Oct. 18. A family fun night at a local farm is Oct. 28 at 4 p.m. The township's annual Diwali event, themed "East meets West," is Sunday, Nov. 1, from 4 to 7 p.m. at the senior center, with cultural programming, food and shopping.

Source: announced at the Oct. 5 council meeting (our recap)
