Around Monroe

Practical news from official sources · Updated Nov 22, 2026

Watch your mailbox for ANCHOR checks

A council member said several residents' state ANCHOR property-tax relief checks were stolen from mailboxes and cashed by someone else. He said the state reissued payment to the residents who reported it, and he asked the township to post an alert.

Source: Nov. 4 council meeting (our recap)

Well 16 is offline while its filter material is replaced

The township's water utility took Well 16 out of service after new treatment media didn't perform as the manufacturer promised. Its director said there is no violation of drinking-water rules and no impact on residents' water use, and that new PFAS treatment is coming to Wells 17 and 19.

Source: Nov. 4 council meeting (our recap)

December meeting dates

Zoning Board Tue., Dec. 1; Town Council Wed., Dec. 2; Planning Board Thu., Dec. 3; Board of Education Wed., Dec. 16. All at 6:30 p.m., except the school board, which opens at 6:15 p.m. with its public portion at 7:15 p.m. at the high school. Dates can change, so check the official calendars.
