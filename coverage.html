What we cover, and what we don't yet

Our goal is every public meeting of Monroe Township's government. We are not there yet, and we would rather show you exactly where the gaps are than imply otherwise. This page is updated whenever we publish. Last updated: October 18, 2026.

Not covered yet

School board committees. The Board of Education's finance, policy, buildings and grounds, curriculum and legislative advocacy committees each meet monthly and post video. We have not recapped them; their reports to the full board are summarized in our board meeting recaps.

Other township bodies. The township's website lists 17 boards, commissions and committees. Besides the Planning Board and Zoning Board, they are the ADA Committee, Affordable Housing Board, Citizens Review Board, Commission on Aging, Cultural Arts Commission, Economic Development Advisory Board, Environmental Commission, Health Advisory Board, Historic Preservation, Human Relations Commission, Library Board of Trustees, Open Space & Farmland Preservation, Recreation Advisory Board, Shade Tree Commission and the Green Team. We have not checked which of these record their meetings, and we do not cover any of them yet. Special meetings and closed sessions are not covered either.

How we decide what counts

We write only from official recordings and transcripts, so a meeting that isn't recorded or isn't posted can't be recapped here, and a meeting appears on this page only after its recap is published. Recaps normally follow within a few days of a recording being posted, but a long meeting or missing captions can delay one. If you know of a meeting we're missing, or one you'd like covered next, tell us by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository.

Meeting dates come from the township's and the school district's published calendars and are subject to change. Check the township's boards page or the school district's meeting dates page before you plan to attend.
