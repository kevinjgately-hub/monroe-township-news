What this is

Local government, in plain language

Towns post hours of video of their council, school board and planning meetings, full of ordinance numbers and procedure. Towns in Focus turns each meeting into a short article that explains the terms as it goes, built from the town's own official recordings and records. We say what we have and have not covered, and we take no side on disputed matters.

Where we are

Monroe in Focus: Monroe Township, Middlesex County, New Jersey

Recaps of the Town Council, Board of Education, Planning Board and Zoning Board, plus a roundup of the township's smaller boards. Every 2026 council meeting so far is covered.
